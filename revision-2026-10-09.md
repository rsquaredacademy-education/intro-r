# Revision Notes — 2026-10-09 (chapter-numbering fix)

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Scope:** Fix off-by-one chapter numbering on every page; render-verified with Quarto 1.6.40
**Author:** automated review pass

Part of a workspace-wide standard for all six books, documented at
[`viz-base/AUTHOR-STANDARDS.md`](https://github.com/rsquaredacademy-publications/viz-base/blob/master/AUTHOR-STANDARDS.md).
This file records only what changed in **this** repository.

## Summary

One change: `index.qmd` shipped bookdown-era YAML (`title:`, `author:`, `date:`,
`lang:`, `documentclass:`, `link-citations:`). The `title:` made the landing
page render as **numbered chapter 1**, so all 13 content chapters rendered as
2–14 while the book's own README counted them as 1–13.

This is the exact defect wave 1 fixed in bash-intro, and the exact failure mode
`viz-base/AUTHORING.md` documents as its first silent failure. The wave-1 review
of *this* repo examined `index.qmd`, saw that it had no `#` H1 competing with the
`title:` (its H1 is `.unnumbered`), and concluded the key was safe on a landing
page. That conclusion was wrong — it was verified against the rendered output
this time, not against the source.

---

## 1. The defect

The committed `docs/index.html` (rebuilt by CI on 2026-10-05, after the last
source change, so current) showed:

- sidebar entry for the landing page: `1  Introduction to R` — not `Preface`
- `introduction-to-r.html`: `2  Introduction to R`
- `install-r-and-rstudio.html`: `3  Install R & RStudio`, … `first-wrangle.html`: `14  Your First Data Wrangling`

A YAML `title:` on a book page renders a title block and claims a chapter number;
`{.unnumbered}` on the markdown H1 does not suppress that. The page therefore
rendered **two** H1s — a numbered title block plus the unnumbered `Preface`.

The other five books carry only `description:` in `index.qmd` front matter and
render the landing page as an unnumbered `Preface`, taking their title block from
`book.title` in `_quarto.yml` instead.

---

## 2. The fix

`index.qmd` front matter reduced to `description:` alone:

```diff
 --- 
-title: "Introduction to R"
-author: "Aravind Hebbali"
-date: "`r Sys.Date()`"
-lang: en
-documentclass: book
-link-citations: true
-description: "A beginner-friendly introduction to the R programming language."
+---
+description: "A beginner-friendly introduction to the R programming language."
 ---
```

`link-citations` was safe to drop: the book contains no `[@...]` citations (it
ships no `.bib`). Title, author and date are all declared in `_quarto.yml: book:`.

---

## Verification

Rendered locally to a scratch directory (not `docs/`, which CI owns):

- Sidebar: `Preface` and `About the Author` unnumbered; chapters renumber to
  **1 Introduction to R … 13 Your First Data Wrangling**.
- The landing page still renders its title block from `book.title`, and a single
  `<h1 class="unnumbered">Preface</h1>` — no second H1.
- All 15 declared chapters render; no errors or warnings.
- The README's "Chapters 11–13 include interactive webR cells" claim is now
  accurate: `projects-import` (11), `first-plot` (12) and `first-wrangle` (13)
  hold the book's three closing WebR playgrounds.
